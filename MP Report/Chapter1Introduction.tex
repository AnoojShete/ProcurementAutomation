\section{Background}
IT procurement covers the acquisition and management of hardware, software licences, SaaS subscriptions and related services. A single purchase passes through several stages: a vendor's documents are received and checked, the request is approved, stock or licences are allocated, a contract is signed and renewed, and the vendor's risk is assessed. In many organisations these stages live in different places---e-mail, spreadsheets, scanned invoices and separate systems---so no one has a current view of a purchase from request to payment.

\section{Motivation}
Manual procurement is slow, and some of its errors are expensive. Retyped invoices introduce mistakes; the same invoice can be paid twice; licences remain paid for long after their users have left; contracts renew silently because a notice period was missed; and a request to change a supplier's bank details, if accepted without independent verification, diverts a payment to a fraudster. These failures share a cause: each stage is handled on its own, and decisions are taken without the evidence held by the other stages. A system that automates the routine steps, links the stages through shared records, and asks a person only when a decision is uncertain or sensitive would address that cause directly.

\section{Aim and Objectives}

\subsection{Aim}
To develop an IT Procurement Intelligence Platform that automates the main activities of the IT procurement lifecycle while keeping critical decisions under human control.

\subsection{Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Extract the key fields of purchase orders, invoices and quotations, with a confidence score for each result.
  \item Match vendors against existing records, validate their tax and bank identifiers, and detect duplicate vendors and invoices.
  \item Route purchase requests through spend-tier approval chains with deadlines, escalation and stock reservation.
  \item Detect under-used software licences and propose their reclamation under human control.
  \item Generate and sign contracts, extract their renewal terms and issue renewal reminders.
  \item Score vendor risk in an explainable and auditable way, and decide whether a vendor is cleared before it is used.
  \item Provide authentication, role-based access, run-time business rules, notifications, monitoring and an audit trail.
  \item Measure each model against a simple baseline, and measure how often the document review gate admits an error.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Problem Statement}
Procurement data and decisions are fragmented across tools, so routine checks---whether an invoice duplicates an earlier one, whether its vendor and bank details are genuine, whether a licence is still used, whether a contract is about to renew---take manual effort and are often skipped. Automating these checks naively creates a different risk: a system that acts on extracted data without a measure of confidence, or without a person in the loop for sensitive changes, makes wrong procurement decisions at scale. The problem is therefore to automate the procurement lifecycle end to end while making every automated decision explainable, auditable and, where it is uncertain, subject to human review. Chapter~\ref{chap3} states the problem in detail.

\section{Scope}
The platform covers the path from an incoming document or purchase request to a signed contract, receipt of the goods and the matching of the vendor's invoice, together with licence reclamation, vendor risk scoring and the shared platform services. It is a decision-support and workflow system: payment execution, ERP integration and production deployment are outside its scope. Section~\ref{sec:scope3} defines the scope precisely.
