Competitive analysis

Based on the 12 products in Scry AI, Best Procurement Automation Software (https://scryai.com/blog/best-procurement-automation-software/), each checked on the vendor's own pages where they could be fetched (20 October 2026). Vendor pages are marketing material; accuracy and adoption figures quoted by vendors are their claims, not verified.

Since this snapshot (21 Oct 2026): the gaps it found were closed by the feature expansion (feature-expansion-plan.md): budget control (Phase 1), contract obligations (Phase 2), IT catalog, RFQs and partial receipts (Phase 3), and Indian GST and TDS: GST check with the signed e-invoice QR, reverse charge, GSTR-2B reconciliation, TDS at booking, MSME deadlines (Phase 4). The tables below are kept as they were found.

1. Choosing the five competitors

Each product was scored on the eight capability areas our platform covers: document capture and extraction, vendor verification and fraud controls, invoice matching, requisitions and approvals, inventory, software licences, contracts and renewals, supplier risk (1 = covered, ½ = partly, 0 = not stated).

Product Score Why
Coupa 7 Every area except licences; 50+ AI agents
Zapro 6 AI intake, AP matching, inventory, contracts, licence tracking
SAP Ariba 5.5 Guided buying, contracts, AI supplier risk, smart OCR, fraud detection
Ivalua 4.5 Full source-to-pay; no AI stated
TYASuite 4 Indian P2P suite: AI invoice capture, GST/TDS, 3-way match
Collatio (Scry AI) 3.5 Document AI and six-way matching only; no purchasing workflow
Procurify 3 Requests, budgets, 2/3-way matching
ZYNO 2.5 SME requisitions, quotations, invoice matching
Precoro, Spendesk, MetaWMS 1.5 Purchasing/approvals, cards, or warehouse only
Rippling 1 Cards, expenses, bills

The five competitors are Coupa, Zapro, SAP Ariba, Ivalua and TYASuite. Collatio is not one of the five, but it is the closest to our document agent and research (matching an invoice against several records), so it is discussed in the research gap.

Top competitor: Coupa. It overlaps with the most areas (7 of 8), has the most mature AI (invoice capture via Rossum, obligation extraction from contracts, supplier-risk and sanctions agents) and adds budgets, sourcing, catalogs, a supplier network and payments. Zapro is the closest peer in size and scope: mid-market, AI intake, contracts with renewal alerts, inventory, and the only other product that tracks software licences.

4. What we have and they don't (per their public material)

  • An error rate for automatic acceptance, measured and controlled: vendors quote extraction accuracy (Coupa 96 %, TYASuite 99 %), not how often a document accepted without review is wrong.
  • Licence-usage anomaly detection with explanations and a grace period; Zapro tracks licences but describes no usage model.
  • Dual control of bank-detail changes and look-alike vendor detection.
  • Evaluated models (clause extraction against rules on CUAD, risk model against its ceiling) with published results.
  • Self-hosted: documents never leave the organisation's machine.

Zoho Procurement (not in the Scry list) is also worth watching: India-built, requisitions to goods receipt, RFQs, contract tracking and agentic AI, per Zoho's own buyer's guide.
