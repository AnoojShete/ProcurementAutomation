# Results and evidence

What to paste into the report's "Results and Evaluation" sections and the
deck ([docs/presentation-outline.md](../presentation-outline.md)): one
sample, one screenshot and the numbers per agent. Everything here is
regenerated by a script named next to it; numbers as of **16 Oct 2026**.
All data is synthetic — these show the system works and how its models
compare with simple baselines, not how they would do on a real company's
documents.
