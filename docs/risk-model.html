How a vendor is rated

A deterministic 100-point ruleset (contract-risk-agent, app/services/scorecard/). Each check runs from a collector or the platform's own history and gives integer points; the same inputs always give the same score, and every score can be replayed.

GroupChecks (max points)Total
Identity and complianceGST registration (20), sanctions (10), bank account (5), jurisdiction (4)39
Operational performanceon-time delivery (15), buyer feedback (12), incidents (8): need ≥ 3 orders / ratings35
Cyber posturewebsite TLS (6), e-mail DMARC/SPF (3), breaches (4, detect-only), certification (4)17
Company standingfinancial rating (4), company age (2)6
Reputationadverse media (3, detect-only)3

Bands: Low 0–24 · Medium 25–49 · High 50+. Hard overrides make a vendor High whatever the points:

RuleWhenCan it be lifted?
R-SAN-01sanctions screening: matchnever by a decision only by resolving it (e.g. an upheld dispute)
R-JUR-01country of incorporation prohibitednever by a decision
R-GST-01/02/03GSTIN cancelled, suspended, not foundremediable reactivation, or an admin's time-boxed accept risk (≤ 30 days)

Rating status: assessed (every applicable check done, required checks for the tier done, country known) · provisional (some outstanding) · not rated (nothing assessed). A vendor that isn't assessed is shown as Unrated, never Low: missing data is excluded, never counted as safe.

Clearance: may this vendor be used?

One decision (app/services/clearance.py) used by GET /vendors/{id}/clearance and by contract signing, in this order:

  1. No score → not cleared.
  2. Non-remediable override (sanctions match, prohibited country) → never cleared, and signing is blocked; an admin can't accept the risk.
  3. The score: other overrides block; not rated blocks; provisional blocks activation, and a purchase unless the vendor is petty tier, the amount ≤ risk.petty_threshold (₹50,000) and no sanctions result awaits review; High blocks.
  4. The latest human decision: reject blocks; approve / approve with conditions count only while the current score is still assessed Low/Medium with no override (and no condition is overdue); accept risk counts until it expires.
  5. Vendor status: purchases and contracts need an active vendor.

The answer lists only the reasons that decided it. Contract signing asks for an acknowledgement exactly when clearance says not cleared.

Retired ML model (for the record)

The first risk model was a Random Forest on six features over 2,000 synthetic vendors: ROC-AUC 0.715 (ceiling 0.739), incident rate Low 6.5 % / Medium 20 % / High 40 %, 12/12 of its own red-team profiles. It was replaced because a procurement decision must be explained and replayed exactly, and a fixed ruleset does that; its code is kept in services/contract-risk-agent/ml/archive/ and its numbers in docs/results/risk-model.json.

Limits

  • External sources (GST registry, TLS grading, DMARC, breach and media feeds) are unreachable in this stack, so live vendors stay provisional / Unrated unless a check is entered as evidence.
  • The evaluation uses synthetic labels; there are no real incident outcomes yet.
  • The red team covers the rules as documented; it can't prove the collectors read real sources correctly.
  • Turning approval.unrated_vendor_requires_finance on routes most purchases to finance until vendors are fully assessed.
